    05 · Notes

    Good to know.

    The key answers before your first order.

    Note 1Is a phone photo of the paper plan enough?+

    Usually yes. Photograph the plan as straight from above as possible, in good light and without shadows. What matters is that the dimensions are readable.

    Note 2May I show the floor plans in listings and on portals?+

    Yes. The floor plans are made for marketing the property: in the listing, on your website and on property portals.

    Note 3How fast is delivery?+

    Usually within 48 hours. For several properties at once I state the date in the quote.

    Note 4Is the area calculation an official survey?+

    No. Areas are determined from the agreed documents, without an on-site survey. No official, expert or legal verification is involved, and the areas are not signed or certified. The figures are without guarantee.

    Note 5How confidentially are my documents handled?+

    I use your documents only for your order and do not pass them on. I only show a property as a reference with your explicit consent.
