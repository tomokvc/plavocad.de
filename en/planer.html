05 · Notes

Notes on the plan.

The key answers before you start. Everything else we sort out directly.

Note 1What templates can I send?+

PDFs, scans, photos of paper plans or existing CAD files. Readable dimensions and, if available, a reference dimension for checking help to get a reliable result.

Note 2Can you work to our layer and drawing standards?+

Yes. Send your layer list, a sample file or short guidelines. I agree the structure with you before starting.

Note 3How is the price calculated?+

The calculator above shows the standard price. After reviewing your documents you get a fixed price before work begins. It will not cost more than agreed.

Note 4How confidentially are my plans handled?+

I use your documents only for your order and do not pass them on. I only show a project as a reference with your explicit consent. On request I sign a non-disclosure agreement.

Note 5Which software do you use?+

I draw in AutoCAD and deliver DWG, DXF and PDF. 3D models are available as IFC or SketchUp.

Note 6How fast can you deliver?+

That depends on scope and workload, usually 3–5 working days. I state the date in the quote so you can plan internally. Feel free to mark urgent requests.

Note 7Is the area calculation an official survey?+

No. Areas are determined from the agreed documents, without an on-site survey. No official, expert or legal verification is involved.
